# PlanSwap icon candidates

Generated using the built-in imagegen tool. PNG exports are 1024 x 1024 pixels. The selected version is [Variant C with a small AI sparkle and opaque navy background](planswap-c-ai-solid.png), copied to [resources/icon.png](../../../resources/icon.png) for the extension manifest and README. Earlier candidates are retained for reference and excluded from the VSIX package. The activity bar uses the matching monochrome [resources/account.svg](../../../resources/account.svg).

## Variant A

Use case: logo-brand.
Design an app icon for a VS Code extension named PlanSwap. It switches between several AI subscription accounts with one click.
Concept: exactly two overlapping rounded membership/subscription cards, slightly offset, surrounded by a pair of circular swap arrows. Front card warm gold (#E0B34A to #F5D27A), back card clear blue (#3B8EEA). Deep navy (#1E2A3A) rounded-square background tile filling the canvas. Centered composition and even padding. Flat, modern, minimal, at most subtle gradient and soft shadow. Bold simple shapes recognizable at 32x32. Excellent contrast on light and dark surroundings.
No text, letters, numerals, brand logos, people, photographic detail, tiny ornamental marks or watermarks. Do not add a chip or star or card writing. Arrows must have clear directional arrowheads and appear as two separated segments of a circular switching path. Keep card silhouettes clearly distinguishable from arrows.
Output one square 1024x1024 PNG app icon, not a presentation board, no mockups, no extra layout.
Variant A: calm balanced geometry. Both cards are level, with blue offset up-left and gold down-right. The cards form a compact central stack. Two substantial gold and blue arc arrows wrap above-right and below-left around the cards, with generous navy gaps separating arcs from cards. Near-flat colors, crisp polished edges, restrained soft shadow.
